The whole game

Three steps. No app to download.

You already know how to do this. You've been collecting stories your whole life — this just gives them a frame and a serial number.

◆

Grab a card

Pick the achievement you're going after. Rain Walker is easy — it rains 150 days a year here. Storm Paddler is not. Choose accordingly.

⚑

Go do the thing

This is the only part we can't do for you. Ride the tram. Eat at the dock. Get properly soaked. Watch an eagle take food directly out of a stranger's hand.

▣

Scan & claim it

Point your phone at the code on the back. Your card unlocks, the foil wakes up, and the date gets written into it. Sign it. Take it home.

The artwork

Every card is a painting.

No stock photos. No clip art. Every single card is an original illustrated scene — the light off Tongass Narrows at 6am, a squall coming over Deer Mountain, the exact shade of green the water goes right before it turns on you. Tilt one and the foil moves.

Not all cards are equal

Six tiers. Most people stop at three.

Anyone can get wet in Ketchikan. Almost nobody survives an outhouse fire. The rarer the card, the louder the foil — and the better the story you have to tell to explain it.

✦
The foil scales with the story

Commons get a whisper of shine. Mythics are a full rainbow that follows your hand across the card.

Serialized, one by one

Every card carries its own number. Yours is not the same as anyone else's, and it never will be.
