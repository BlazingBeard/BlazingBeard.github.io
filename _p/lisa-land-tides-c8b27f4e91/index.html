A FEW THINGS THE FISH WON'T TELL YOU

A little patience.
A little give and take.

  1. Find your fish.

    Tap the water near a moving shadow to cast. Or aim with the arrow keys and press Space. A cast close to a fish gets its attention.

  2. Don't fall for the nibble.

    Small bobs are only a tease. Wait for the big splash and “Hook it!”, then tap or press Space. Too early or too late, and it swims away.

  3. Let it fight. Then reel.

    Hold the button or Space to bring it in. When a fish runs or dives, let go to ease the line. Reel during its rest. Keep tension out of both ends: a tight line snaps, a slack line loses the hook.

  4. A memory, not a deadline.

    Every catch adds a memory to your field notes. Release it when you're ready. Five species have different fights. Your collection saves on this browser, independently of the original game.
