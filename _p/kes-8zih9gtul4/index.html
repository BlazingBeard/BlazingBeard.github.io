Meet the artist

Painted at the kitchen table, between rainstorms.

I live on an island in Southeast Alaska where it rains about 150 inches a year. Everything here is green and gray. So I paint it bright: lavender seas, coral tentacles, blue bears with wavy-grain fur, and the fern-spiral blossom that ends up in every painting.

Everything starts as an acrylic painting on canvas. Prints are made from scans of the originals, then signed by hand and shipped flat in rigid mailers.

— KES

Good to know

Questions

Are the prints signed?

Yes. Every print is signed by hand. Originals are also signed and dated on the back, with the name of the painting.

How do you ship art from an island?

Prints ship flat between rigid boards, inside a weatherproof sleeve, by USPS with tracking. Originals ship insured. Alaska mail is slower than you'd expect, so add a few days.

I visited on a cruise and saw your work. Can I still buy it?

Yes. Everything from the booth is here, and anything sold out can usually be printed again. Message me if you're looking for something specific.

What is an Alaska Card?

A collectible trading card with one of my paintings on the front and a real Ketchikan experience on the back. Scan the QR code to claim it and add it to your deck. Packs are coming soon.

Returns?

If a print arrives damaged, send a photo and I'll replace it. Originals and commissions are one of a kind, so please message me before you buy if you're unsure.
